CHANGES IN THIS VERSION
=======================
1. Tree trunk (world.js): same shape, 40% slimmer (TS = .6). Roots follow. Giri (engine.js) now sits
   against the trunk instead of inside it; his blanket, cigarette pack and the rucksack were moved with him.
   The rucksack now leans on the trunk. To fine tune: change TS in world.js and gs/seat in engine.js.
2. Sound (engine.js):
   - Ambient button now really toggles (before, "on" never changed so it did nothing).
   - New soft open sounds for every object, and a new CLOSE sound when a panel is closed.
   - Missing sounds added (page turn, butterfly). All effects use soft plucks and paper "air" in the
     same key as the ambience, through one gentle filter, so they stay quiet and unharsh.
   - Sounds wait for the first tap or key, so nothing bursts out when the page unlocks audio.
3. claude-demo.html: the whole site in one file for previewing. index.html is still the real entry.

ROUND 2
=======
4. Coffee cup (world.js): the old cup was a solid block with a flat brown disc buried inside it, so the
   coffee was hidden/flickering and the cup looked empty. It is now a hollow ceramic cup with real liquid:
   a level that rises and falls, and a creamy spiral that slowly drifts (faster while pouring).
   The cup also sat out of Giri's reach and the cat could walk through it. It now sits on a flat "tea rock"
   beside him, together with a new kettle.
5. Drinking (engine.js, teaUpd): every 9 to 21 seconds (only when the smoking animation is idle and no panel is
   open) Giri reaches with his left arm, lifts the cup to his lips, tips it and sips (4 sips per cup).
   When it is empty he picks up the kettle, pours a visible stream into the cup (level rises, steam,
   pouring sound), puts the kettle back, and starts sipping again. Arms use two-bone inverse kinematics.
6. Epilogue: after every object has been opened once and the last panel is closed, a closing line appears
   and the lantern glows for a moment. Set your own line with  epilogue: "..."  inside CONTENT in content.js.
7. index.html had no <title> or description. Added both (tab name, search results, link previews).
